# Portfolio Requirements Gate

Elite Estimating is the portfolio's multi-domain estimating and automotive/property intelligence platform and a primary InsurTech/AutoTech R&D commercialization asset.

## Required gates
- Data-neutral estimate kernel with documented calculations, revision history, provenance, and configurable rules.
- Licensed/authorized provider adapters only; no unauthorized scraping, copying, or redistribution of proprietary estimating, parts, labor, OEM, or procedure databases.
- Safety-critical structural, restraint, ADAS/calibration, EV/HV, and repair-procedure recommendations require source-backed evidence and human review.
- AI damage/repair recommendations must expose confidence/uncertainty, model/version, input provenance, and reviewer override; AI must never silently replace approved estimate lines.
- Multi-tenant isolation, RBAC, audit trails, secure file/photo handling, secrets management, rate limiting, backups, rollback, observability, and incident response.
- Interoperability through standards and licensed connectors; preserve provider-neutral boundaries.
- Cross-platform inspection/review workflows for web, field/mobile, and desktop-compatible delivery where practical.
- Commercial metrics: estimates processed, estimate time, supplement rate, reviewer override, accuracy/agreement metrics, cycle time, cost per estimate, customer/pilot usage, ACV/MRR/ARR, gross margin, retention, and integration cost.
- R&D/grants: document hypotheses, technical uncertainty, baselines, experiments, held-out evaluation, measurable milestones, and commercialization for multimodal damage intelligence, uncertainty, procedure reasoning, data federation, or other genuinely novel work.
- University collaborations must define background/foreground IP, data rights, publication, confidentiality, licensing, and commercialization before research begins.
- Diligence: architecture, data/provider rights, model/evaluation evidence, security posture, IP ownership, dependency licenses, customer validation, competitive matrix, roadmap, financial model, and use-of-funds.

## Release rule
Do not claim parity/superiority to proprietary estimating systems, autonomous repair decisions, or production accuracy without reproducible benchmark and customer evidence.
