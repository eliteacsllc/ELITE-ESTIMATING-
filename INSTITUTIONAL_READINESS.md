# Institutional Readiness

Classification: Vertical Product / Automotive and Multi-Domain Estimating Intelligence / R&D candidate.

Institutional readiness requires evidence across repository controls, live deployment, technical validation, commercialization, and funding diligence.

## Repository controls
Required: build/typecheck/test; smoke and migration verification; multi-tenant authorization; auditability; provider-neutral adapters; provenance for every external datum; AI confidence and human approval; safety-critical OEM/ADAS evidence; secrets/dependency controls; backup/recovery; observability; interoperability contracts.

## Technical validation
Maintain reproducible evaluation sets and versioned metrics for damage/component recognition inputs, estimate recommendation quality, source/procedure retrieval accuracy, confidence calibration, human override rate, and failure modes. Do not publish accuracy claims without measured evidence and dataset rights.

## Data and IP
No unauthorized scraping, copying, or redistribution of proprietary estimating databases or protected procedures. Record source/provider, license/right to use, retrieval time, region, version, confidence, and redistribution restrictions. Track third-party code/model licenses and ownership of original code, schemas, prompts, rules, and evaluation assets.

## Commercial validation
Track estimate cycle-time reduction, revision/supplement rate, line-item acceptance/override rate, cost per estimate, gross margin, customer retention, and enterprise integration milestones. Internal validation and independent external validation must be reported separately.

## Funding/university readiness
For SBIR/STTR/university proposals, identify genuine unresolved technical risks, hypotheses, baselines, experiments, success thresholds, commercialization path, and research-institution work allocation where applicable. Ordinary SaaS engineering is not to be represented as research.

## Portfolio contract
Claims Management consumes estimate outputs and workflows. Vehicle Photo Labeler supplies permissioned visual intelligence. Echelon supplies reusable orchestration/authorization. This repo owns the estimate-domain kernel, provenance, rules, and estimating interfaces.

## Status labels
Use only: PROTOTYPE, REPOSITORY-READY, DEPLOYMENT-VERIFIED, TECHNICALLY-VALIDATED, CUSTOMER-VALIDATED, REVENUE-VALIDATED, or INSTITUTIONALLY-READY. Every label requires auditable evidence.
