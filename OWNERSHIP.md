# Ownership and Control Register

Repository steward: `@eliteacsllc` for `eliteacsllc/ELITE-ESTIMATING-`; operational code ownership is defined by `.github/CODEOWNERS`.

Production-critical resources—repository access, authorized data-provider contracts/accounts, databases/storage, deployment infrastructure, domains, payment services where enabled, backups and recovery credentials—must be business-controlled or covered by documented transferable authorization.

No critical production dependency may rely on an undocumented personal account, unrecoverable secret or non-transferable provider entitlement. Secrets stay outside Git. Legal rights to estimating/repair data, models, assets and integrations are governed by `IP_PROVENANCE.md`; unknown ownership or rights block release.
